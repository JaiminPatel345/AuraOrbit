AuraOrbit is a next-generation Android Live Wallpaper featuring a fully interactive 3D sphere that orbits your favorite apps right on your home screen. Built on the high-performance libGDX game engine, it leverages a golden angle Fibonacci distribution to plot your apps perfectly in a 3D space, supporting true 120 FPS hardware refresh rates!

<b>Core Features:</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Seamless 3D Rotation:</b> Touch and drag anywhere on your home screen to rotate the sphere along multiple axes.</li>
<li><b>Instant App Launching:</b> Tap any orbiting app icon to launch it immediately without delay.</li>
<li><b>Smart Dynamic Widgets:</b> Color-coded widgets bound to custom app groups that update in real-time.</li>
<li><b>Deep Personalization:</b> Set target framerates (60/90/120), toggle system backgrounds, and adjust sphere radii and icon scaling.</li>
<li><b>FOSS &amp; Privacy First:</b> Entirely open source and requests zero unnecessary permissions.</li>
</ul>
