AuraOrbit is a next-generation Android Live Wallpaper featuring a fully interactive 3D sphere that orbits your favorite apps right on your home screen. Built on the high-performance libGDX game engine, it leverages a golden angle Fibonacci distribution to plot your apps perfectly in a 3D space, supporting true 120 FPS hardware refresh rates!

<b>3D Sphere:</b>
<ul>
<li>Rotate apps with natural quaternion-based momentum at up to 120 FPS.</li>
<li>Drag-and-drop sphere positioning — center, top, bottom, or anywhere via a custom editor (which also scales the sphere radius!)</li>
<li>Adjustable icon size and rotation speed (sphere maintains full radius regardless of icon size).</li>
</ul>

<b>Background &amp; Blur:</b>
<ul>
<li>Upload a custom background image or use the default gradient.</li>
<li>Dual blur controls: independent Blur Radius (area) and Blur Strength (intensity) with real-time preview.</li>
<li>Five blur levels from no blur to full-screen blur.</li>
</ul>

<b>App Groups &amp; Widgets:</b>
<ul>
<li>Create color-coded app groups with 8 presets plus a custom RGB color picker.</li>
<li><b>Individual Group Overrides:</b> Configure unique Sphere Position, Background Image, and Blur settings exclusively for each group.</li>
<li>Intuitive Edit Apps popup dialog for seamless bulk-selection.</li>
<li>Per-group home screen widgets with live previews and custom logo upload.</li>
<li>Toggle transparent background, hide logo, hide text, or use Material You system colors per widget.</li>
<li>Pin multiple widgets for different groups simultaneously.</li>
</ul>

<b>Standalone Sphere Mode:</b>
<ul>
<li>Launch AuraOrbit as a fullscreen immersive app from any group widget.</li>
<li>Floats seamlessly over your home screen with zero black dimming!</li>
<li>Tap apps to launch them; tap outside to return home.</li>
</ul>

<b>FOSS &amp; Privacy First:</b>
<ul>
<li>Entirely open source under GPLv3.</li>
<li>Zero unnecessary permissions and no tracking whatsoever.</li>
</ul>
